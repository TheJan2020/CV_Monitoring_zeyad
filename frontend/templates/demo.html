Activate your laptop or phone camera in the browser. Each frame is sent over the Cloudflare tunnel to the GPU box, analysed by YOLO11s, and the resulting bounding boxes are drawn back onto the overlay below. Inference happens on a shared NVIDIA Quadro RTX 5000. No frames are stored — every request is processed and discarded.

How this works
  1. The browser uses getUserMedia to access your camera (you'll see a permission prompt).
  2. Every ~250 ms (4 fps default) a frame is drawn to a hidden canvas, encoded as JPEG ~70 % quality.
  3. The JPEG is POSTed to /api/demo/analyze over HTTPS through the Cloudflare tunnel.
  4. The hub runs YOLO11s on the GPU at 640 px imgsz, conf ≥ 0.25, and returns the bounding boxes as JSON.
  5. The overlay canvas draws the boxes on top of the live video.
