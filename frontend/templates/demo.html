Activate your laptop or phone camera in the browser.     How this works
    1. The browser uses getUserMedia to access your camera (you'll see a permission prompt).
    2. Every ~250 ms (4 fps default) a frame is drawn to a hidden canvas, encoded as JPEG ~70 % quality.
    3. The JPEG is POSTed to /api/demo/analyze over HTTPS through the Cloudflare tunnel.
    4. The hub runs YOLO11s on the GPU at 640 px imgsz, conf ≥ 0.25, plus every trained custom-class model (IQOS etc.), and returns the bounding boxes as JSON. When the pose toggle is on, yolo11s-pose also runs and returns COCO 17 keypoints per person.
    5. The overlay canvas draws boxes for the classes you've enabled and (optionally) the yellow skeleton on top of the live video.
